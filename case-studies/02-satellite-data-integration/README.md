# Satellite Data Integration for Exploration

**Sentinel-2 / ASTER | Sudan | Public methodology summary**

[Back to portfolio](../../README.md)

## Objective and methods

The existing bilingual satellite-integration reports describe the use of Sentinel-2 and ASTER imagery to examine surface alteration indicators associated with exploration.

Their methodology discusses atmospheric correction, resampling, cloud and shadow masking, spectral ratios, PCA, MNF and DEM-derived lineaments. Comparative imagery is used to discuss changes in surface responses between dates.

## Outputs and transferable skills

- Spectral composites and alteration-response interpretations.
- Comparison of complementary satellite datasets.
- Historical and recent image interpretation.
- Technical explanation of preprocessing and evidence integration.

## Evidence boundary

The source describes hypothetical ground-truth inputs in its methodology. Its numerical classification claims are therefore not adopted here as independently validated performance. Surface patterns and extraction activity are not proof of ore grade or subsurface mineralization.

This summary omits coordinates, local study-area names, client identifiers and analytical maps. The original reports remain intact at their previous repository paths; their inventory is recorded in the regional report catalogue.
