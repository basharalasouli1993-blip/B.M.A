# Alteration Mapping in a Vegetated Environment

**Ghana | Bilingual technical reporting | Public project summary**

[Back to portfolio](../../README.md)

## Objective

Investigate alteration-related surface signatures in a vegetated environment and communicate the limitations of medium-resolution satellite interpretation.

## Documented approach

The English and Arabic reports explain spectral ratios and composite interpretation, potential iron-oxide and hydroxyl-bearing responses, vegetation interference and NDVI masking considerations.

## Outputs and transferable skills

- Figure-led spectral interpretation and technical reporting.
- Consideration of land-cover interference when comparing surface responses.
- Recommendations for field validation and interpretation controls.
- Parallel Arabic and English reporting for different audiences.

## Evidence boundary and public scope

Gold is not directly identified from these multispectral signatures. The analysis concerns alteration proxies and follow-up investigation. This public summary does not claim verified deposits or independently measured classification accuracy.
