## Preservation and organization

All 15 original PDF-named files are retained at their existing paths. Regional folders provide catalogues, while case-study folders provide readable summaries. The full advanced study is organized through stable release links; its two language editions form one project.

Reports with explicit coordinates, study-boundary links or insufficient image-level privacy review are not copied into new public paths or linked from featured navigation. These folders are public catalogues, not private storage: preserving originals does not make existing sensitive files private.
