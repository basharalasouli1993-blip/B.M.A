## Case D - Evaluating a possible corridor extension

[English report](04_Exploration_Case_D_EN.pdf) · [التقرير العربي](04_Exploration_Case_D_AR.pdf)

Integrated review of recorded soil-gold results, geological/structural context, archived magnetic data and historical imagery. Among 112 sampled locations within a 2 km review radius, four meet the study's 0.055 ppm reference level. The two strongest recorded soil results (0.195 and 0.155 ppm) form a local pair west of the review centre; the neighbourhood median is 0.008 ppm.

**Decision supported:** retain as a fourth-priority extension case and check the paired results, corridor continuity, and possible disturbance or transported material. The review partly overlaps Case A and is not an independent discovery.

## Case E - Balancing conflicting exploration evidence

[English report](05_Exploration_Case_E_EN.pdf) · [التقرير العربي](05_Exploration_Case_E_AR.pdf)

Evidence-weighting example combining soil results, geological context, magnetic contrast and historical image comparison. Among 57 sampled locations within 2 km, only one reaches the 0.055 ppm reference level (0.062 ppm); the neighbourhood median is 0.0025 ppm. Limited mapped local structural support and weaker later surface association constrain the case despite marked magnetic variability.

**Decision supported:** retain as a fifth-priority reserve case. Do not advance to drilling on the present evidence; first repeat the soil result and seek independent local geological support.
